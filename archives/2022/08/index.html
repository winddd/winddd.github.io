Jian Zhang

Software Engineer @ Google

  • Google Scholar

I am a software engineer at Google, working on LLM runtime systems. I received my PhD in Computer Science from Khoury College of Computer Sciences at Northeastern University, advised by Cheng Tan. I was also privileged to work closely with Shuai Mu. My main interests are in LLM systems, system verification, and distributed systems. I’m particularly interested in:

  • Building efficient LLM runtime systems.
  • Verifying correctness of distributed systems.
