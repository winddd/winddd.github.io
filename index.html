Jian Zhang

Software Engineer at Google · LLM Runtime Systems

I am a software engineer at Google CoreML, working on LLM runtime systems. I received my PhD in Computer Science from Khoury College of Computer Sciences at Northeastern University, advised by Cheng Tan. I was also privileged to work closely with Shuai Mu.

Research

My main interests are in LLM systems, system verification, and distributed systems. I’m particularly interested in:

  • Building efficient LLM runtime systems.
  • Verifying correctness of distributed systems.
